Questions

Before you ask.

Is this made with AI?+

Yes. We use AI video tools, then edit and check every frame by hand. We build from your real product photos and keep the product exactly as it is.

Who owns the videos?+

Once you've paid in full, all rights we hold in the final ads transfer to you, and you can use them anywhere with no time limit. We never reuse your product, photos or ads for anyone else, and we may show them in our portfolio unless you tell us not to.

Can you guarantee sales or ROAS?+

No one can honestly guarantee ad results. You get several hooks and angles to find a winner quickly, and monthly clients get the next batch shaped by their numbers.

Which products are a good fit?+

Pet products that show what they do on camera: grooming tools, hair removers, toys, feeders, fountains, beds and travel gear. We will tell you honestly if yours isn't a fit, for example anything that relies on medical claims.

Where are you based?+

Asia (UTC+8). Send your materials during your day and the work happens overnight. We reply to every email within 12 hours.
